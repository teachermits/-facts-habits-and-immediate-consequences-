Grammar: The Zero Conditional

We use the Zero Conditional to talk about facts, habits, and immediate consequences that are always true.

If / When + Subject + Present Simple , Subject + Present Simple

Examples for daily adult life:

  • If I work late, I feel tired the next day.
  • When a client calls, I answer professionally.
  • If you don't pay the electric bill, the company cuts the power.

Reading: David's Routine

"Meet David. He is a busy accountant with a strict routine. If he wakes up early, he goes to the gym. If he doesn't exercise, he feels sluggish all day at the office. He also manages his money carefully. If he saves 10% of his salary, he feels secure about his future. However, if he works late to finish reports, he orders takeout food. This is bad for his health and his wallet. When the weekend arrives, he finally relaxes. If he rests completely on Sunday, he starts Monday with a lot of energy. Our actions always have immediate consequences."
